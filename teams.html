Participating Teams

Twelve of the world's best VALORANT teams have earned their place at VCT Masters London 2026, representing the Americas, EMEA, Pacific, and China regions in a battle for international glory and crucial Championship Points.

Americas

NRG

NRG was the final team to qualify for VALORANT Masters London 2026. They lost their flex player, som, that helped them won VCT Champions 2025 and replaced him with Team Liquid's star player Keiko. After a horrendous start in Stage 1, they made a lower-bracket run before securing their spot at the event.

  • Ethan “Ethan” Cole Arnold
  • Adam “mada” Pampuch
  • Brock “brawk” Somerhalder
  • Logan “skuba” Jenkins
  • Georgio “keiko” Sanassy
  • Malkolm “bonkar” Rench (Head Coach)
  • Mitchell “mitch” Semago (Assistant Coach)

Leviathan

Leviatán has been one of the surprises of the season, blending veteran experience with four rising prodigies. With a promising young roster for the 2026 season, the team secured a dominant victory over MIBR to qualify for Masters London, marking the international debut for most of the roster.

  • Francisco “kiNgg” Alberto Aravena
  • Eduardo “Sato” Kenzo Nagahama Sato
  • Guilherme “blowz” Oliveira
  • Bruno “Neon” Rodríguez
  • Rodrigo “spikeziN” Lombardi

G2 Esports

As for G2 Esports, they continue to deliver strong performances on stage, securing their seventh international qualification. After finishing second at the Masters Bangkok and placing fourth in both Toronto and Santiago, the team remains hungry to finally bring home a trophy. They have qualified as the first seed of the region.

  • Trent “trent” Cairns
  • Jacob “valyn” Batio
  • Nathan “leaf” Orf
  • Alexander “jawgemo” Mor
  • Andrej “babybay” Francisty
  • Josh “JoshRT” Lee (Head Coach)
  • Peter “shhhack” Belej (Assistant Coach)

EMEA

Team Vitality

Considered one of the “superteams” of the season, Team Vitality had a rough start in the Group Stage. Despite barely making it through, they found their form in the Playoffs, securing wins over Team Liquid and FNATIC. They will now face FUT Esports in the Upper Bracket Final.

  • Nikita “Derke” Sirmitev
  • Ștefan “Sayonara” Mîtcu
  • Timofey “Chronicle” Khromov
  • Elias “Jamppi” Olkkonen
  • Dawid “PROFEK” Świę
  • Gregor “PAL” Morton (Head Coach)
  • Benjamin “Scuttt” Hutchinson (Assistant Coach)

FUT Esports

FUT Esports is coming back to the international stage after an incredible performance during the VCT EMEA 2026: Stage 1. After defeating Eternal Fire in the Upper Semifinals by 2-1, the organization is returning to the international stage after two years. Their last appearance was at VALORANT Champions 2024.

Team Heretics

Not only did Team Heretics qualify for Masters London through a complete lower bracket run, but they also defeated Team Vitality to win the VCT EMEA 2026: Stage 1 title. This was the first time the team secured a regional title. They are heading to the event as Europe's first seed.

Paper Rex

The final qualification spot came down to a high-stakes elimination match between T1 and Paper Rex, representing the Masters Bangkok winners against the Masters Toronto winners for a place at VALORANT Masters London. Paper Rex ultimately came out stronger, defeating T1 and securing the final spot. The result not only eliminated the Korean organization but also ended a long-running streak of Korean representation at international VCT events dating back to the circuit's earliest competitions.

With a dominant 13–0 result in the series, they’ve firmly established themselves as one of the top teams in the Chinese region. The team has not lost a single game throughout Stage 1.
